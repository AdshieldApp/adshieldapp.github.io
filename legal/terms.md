## 1. What the app does

AdShield filters DNS lookups on your device to block domains known for advertising, tracking,
malware and phishing, using a local VPN created with Android's `VpnService`. Some ads (for example ads
served from the same domain as the content, such as video ads on some platforms) can't be blocked by
DNS filtering, and **no blocking is guaranteed to be complete**.
