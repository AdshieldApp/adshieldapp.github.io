How AdShield works

A small, local VPN that only handles DNS. Nothing is routed through a remote server.

An app looks up an address

Before loading an ad or tracker, an app asks for its address (a DNS lookup).

AdShield checks it on your phone

The lookup is compared against your blocklists and your own rules, entirely on the device.

Blocked or answered

Ads and trackers get an empty answer and never load. Everything else goes to the DNS provider you chose.

App asks for news.example.com→Resolved normally

Questions, answered

Do I need to root my phone?

No. AdShield uses Android's built-in VPN feature, so it works on regular phones running Android 8.0 or newer.

Does AdShield see my browsing?

AdShield only sees DNS lookups (domain names apps ask for), and it checks them on your phone. It doesn't see page content, doesn't decrypt HTTPS, and doesn't upload your DNS history anywhere.

Will it block every ad, including YouTube ads?

No, and we won't pretend otherwise. DNS filtering can't remove ads served from the same domain as the content, such as YouTube video ads or some social feeds. It's very effective against third-party ad and tracking networks.

Can I use it with another VPN?

Android runs one VPN at a time, so AdShield can't run alongside another VPN app. If another VPN takes over, AdShield can alert you that protection stopped.

Something stopped working. What do I do?

Open the query log or "Recently blocked", tap the domain and allow it. You can also exclude a specific app from filtering, or pause protection for a few minutes.

Is AdShield free?

Yes. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no account.
